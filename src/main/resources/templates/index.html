About Krishna Museum

Located in Kurukshetra, the Krishna Museum is dedicated to the life and teachings of Lord Krishna. It showcases sculptures, paintings, manuscripts, and artefacts from various periods reflecting Indian mythology and culture.

Museum Floors

Floor 1

Sculptures of Krishna, Vishnu, and ancient deities.

Floor 2

Paintings and manuscripts from Mahabharata and Bhagavad Gita.

Floor 3

Coins, ritual objects, and historical items.
